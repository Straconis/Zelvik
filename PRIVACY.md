# Zelvik Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: August 14, 2026**

Zelvik is designed with privacy in mind.

## Data Collection

Zelvik does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information or user data.

Zelvik does not use analytics, telemetry, advertising trackers, or other tracking technologies.

## Local Operation

Zelvik operates locally on the user's computer. Audio processing and application settings remain on the user's device unless the user independently chooses to use Zelvik with a third-party service.

## Third-Party Services

Zelvik may be used alongside third-party applications or services, such as Discord. Zelvik does not control the privacy practices of those services. Use of third-party services is subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

## Changes to This Policy

If Zelvik's data practices change in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe those changes.

## Contact

Questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy may be submitted through the Zelvik GitHub repository.
